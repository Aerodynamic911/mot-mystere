Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Quizzle is a mobile game published by Occitan Games. This page explains, simply, what the app does with your data.

In short

You can play Quizzle without an account: in that case nothing is sent to a server and your progress stays on your phone. If you choose a nickname to play with friends, an anonymous online profile is created and some data is then stored on our servers, as described below. There are no ads, no advertising tracking and no sale of data.

What is stored on your device

  • your progress (levels, event steps, cards, jokers, ice cubes, coins);
  • your game statistics (games, wins, streaks);
  • your preferences (language, difficulty, age group).

This information is stored in the app’s storage, on your phone. It disappears if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The online profile (optional)

The online profile is created only when you choose a nickname in the “My profile” screen. There is no e-mail address, phone number or real name: a random identifier is generated on your phone. The server keeps only a hash of your secret code. The following is then stored:

  • your nickname, and a friend code you may share;
  • your result for the daily mystery (number of tries, success, score), visible to your friends and to the members of your groups;
  • your friend requests, friends, groups, challenges and reactions, and the players you block or report;
  • if you turn on notifications: a notification token specific to your phone, and your language.

This data is used only to make these features work (seeing your friends, challenges, leaderboards, notifications) and to moderate nicknames: nicknames may be reviewed by the publisher, and a nickname reported by several players is hidden. It is kept as long as your profile exists.

Notifications

Notifications (friend request received, request accepted, challenge received) are optional: the app asks for your permission and you can turn them off at any time from “My profile” or from your phone’s settings. They are delivered through Expo’s notification service and then through Apple’s (iPhone) or Google’s (Android) notification services.

Deleting your online data

The “Delete my online data” button in the “My profile” screen immediately erases your profile and all associated data on our servers (nickname, results, friends, groups, challenges, reactions, notification token). You can also write to contact@occitangames.com.

Technical providers

  • Cloudflare (hosting of the server, the database and this website): like any host, it technically processes connection IP addresses;
  • Expo, Apple and Google, only to deliver notifications.

No data is sold or used for advertising.

What we do not do

  • no ads and no advertising tracking;
  • no analytics tool in the app;
  • no reading of your address book, your location or your photos.

Sharing and rating

The share buttons open your phone’s share menu: a message is sent only if you choose an app and a recipient. The optional prompt to rate the app uses the window provided by the App Store or Google Play; the publisher does not receive your rating as personal data.

Ads and purchases

The current version contains no ads and no purchases with real money. If this changes, this policy will be updated before the new version is released, and payments will go through the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment information themselves.

Minimum age

Quizzle is intended for people aged 13 and over.

This website

This website uses no tracking cookies and no analytics. Fonts are hosted on the site itself.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data: the nickname can be changed in the app, and deletion is done from “My profile” or by writing to contact@occitangames.com. You may also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Changes

This policy may change as the app evolves. The update date is shown at the top of this page.
