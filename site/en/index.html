    Three ways to play

    A few minutes is all it takes. Come back every day, or chain levels together.

    The daily mystery

    One mystery a day, the same for everyone. Solve it three days in a row to earn a joker, then your streak keeps growing.

    The adventure

    A treasure map, eight settings and endless levels. Every 30 levels, you earn a new title.

    The events

    Every month, a themed adventure in 20 steps, with a collectible card at the end.

    Riddles for every generation

    Over 200 riddles, from the Eiffel Tower to Minecraft. Pick your age group in the settings: you get the riddles everyone knows, plus the ones that speak to your era.

    • All ages

      Eiffel Tower / Panda / Printing press / Cleopatra

    • Teens, 13 to 17

      Minecraft / Pokémon / Naruto / Usain Bolt

    • 18 to 30

      ChatGPT / Netflix / Daft Punk / Inception

    • 30 to 50

      Tamagotchi / Nokia 3310 / France 98 / Minitel

    • 50 and over

      Apollo 11 / Édith Piaf / 2CV / Tintin

    The game is designed for ages 13 and over.

    Challenge your friends

    After each game, send a code to a friend. They play the same riddle as you and see if they do better. No account to create: the code is all it takes.
